How this works

Press the button and you get one card. Do the small thing. Not feeling it? Draw another. There are no wrong cards.

  • In bed: works lying down, eyes closed, nothing to get up for.
  • Evening: for the hour before bed, a few steps or a light.
  • Needs a thing: a glass, a pen, paper, a blanket.

Five suits of 16: Breathe, Body, Mind, Room and Night (for when you wake up). Each card takes from 1 to 15 minutes. Go slowly.

Sleep Wind-Down Deck is a set of small, calm ideas for winding down. It is not medical advice or treatment, and it does not cure insomnia or any sleep problem. If trouble sleeping lasts more than a few weeks, or you feel very low or anxious, please talk to your doctor.

No account, no tracking. The only thing saved is your "In bed only" switch, on this device.
